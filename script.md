# "The One Bar": 60 s spec ad for PORCHLIGHT MOBILE (a fictional carrier). Chroma Awards S2, Ad > Telecom.
Single benefit: coverage that reaches the end of the road. Human angle: a mother's daily walk up a hill to get one bar.

VO = the grown-up daughter (warm, fond, a little wry). Final line = announcer.

| # | Shot (felt stop-motion) | VO |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wide: Mum in yellow raincoat walking up the hill path, sheep, sunset | D1: "Every evening at seven, my mum walked up Carrow Hill." |
| 2 | Close: felt hand pulls an old phone from raincoat pocket, signal bars empty | D2: "Not for the view. For the one bar." |
| 3 | Hilltop: arm stretched to the sky holding the phone up by the lone tree | D3: "Up there, if she stood on the left side of the tree and held it really high, she could just about hear me." |
| 4 | Rain: same climb with an umbrella | D4: "She climbed it in the rain." |
| 5 | Snow: same climb with a lantern | D5: "In the snow. On her birthday." |
| 6 | Felt workers raise a small mast on the far ridge, a warm lamp on top | D6: "Then last spring, someone put a little light on the next ridge." |
| 7 | Evening: halfway up the path, phone ringing in her pocket, she stops surprised | D7: "The first night, her phone rang halfway up. She nearly dropped it." |
| 8 | At her garden gate laughing on the phone, sheep around | (laugh / music swell) |
| 9 | Hilltop again at sunset, holding phone out on video call to show the view | D8: "She still walks up Carrow Hill. Now she takes me with her." |
| 10 | End card: dusk hills, lamp glowing on the mast. PORCHLIGHT MOBILE | A1: "Porchlight Mobile. Coverage that reaches the end of the road." + "You can still walk up the hill. You just don't have to." |
